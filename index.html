❓ FAQ

How does voice control work?

Tap the VOICE button and say the team name (DELTA, OMEGA, etc.) to assign a point. The app announces the score automatically.

What's the best setup?

Use AirPods or any Bluetooth earbuds/speaker with microphone. Keep your phone in your pocket or bag while playing.

Which languages are supported?

Italian, English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, German, and Finnish.

Does it work offline?

Yes! Once installed, the app works without internet connection.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to the app's home screen and the purchase will be automatically restored if you're logged in with the same Apple ID.

🔒 Privacy

PadelVoice Pro does NOT collect any personal data. Voice audio is processed locally on your device and is never recorded or sent to any server.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Summary: PadelVoice Pro does NOT collect, store, or share any personal data. All data stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

PadelVoice Pro does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not collect device identifiers
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services

2. Microphone Usage

PadelVoice Pro uses your device's microphone for voice recognition to detect score commands during matches.

  • Audio is processed locally on your device
  • Audio is never recorded or stored
  • Audio is never sent to any server
  • Voice recognition uses Apple's built-in Speech Recognition framework

3. Data Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Team names and player names (customizable by you)
  • Language preference
  • Voice settings
  • Match counter for free trial tracking
  • Purchase status (premium/free)

This data never leaves your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

4. In-App Purchases

PadelVoice Pro offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock unlimited matches. All payment processing is handled securely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

5. Third-Party Services

PadelVoice Pro does not use any third-party services that collect data, including:

  • No analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • No advertising networks
  • No social media integrations
  • No crash reporting services

6. Children's Privacy

PadelVoice Pro does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
